five fives at fuckass

Five Fives at Fuckass is a shitty FNaF fangame that we made for fun. It was originally made in Scratch by DroidTA and Oliver before being recoded in the more traditional engine for FNaF fangames, Clickteam Fusion.

The game was made as a parody of bad FNaF fangames while also being a sort of gift to the max-mode community. It was also just a fun thing for our friend group to work on :)

The game was released on New Years Eve 2025 at 11:59pm. This (obviously) was a joke about the game's release date just being "December."
